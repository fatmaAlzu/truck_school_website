WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Next Gear Driving Academy

Next Gear Driving Academy provides professional driver training in Calgary, Alberta for students pursuing Class 1, Class 3, Class 5 and Air Brake training.

Our goal is to help every student develop safe driving habits, practical skills and confidence behind the wheel. Whether you're beginning your driving journey, upgrading your licence or preparing for a career in commercial driving, we're here to help you move forward.

We believe quality driver education goes beyond preparing for a road test. Our training focuses on developing responsible drivers who understand their vehicles, recognize risks and are prepared for real-world driving.
