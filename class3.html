About Class 3

An Alberta Class 3 driver's licence allows you to operate a single motor vehicle with three or more axles, including many straight trucks, dump trucks, large delivery trucks, and other commercial vehicles.

Our Class 3 driver training is designed to help students develop the knowledge and practical driving skills needed to safely operate larger commercial vehicles and prepare for the Alberta Class 3 road test.

What Can You Drive With a Class 3 Licence?

An Alberta Class 3 licence allows you to operate:

  • Single motor vehicles with three or more axles
  • Vehicles normally permitted under a Class 5 licence
  • A three-or-more-axle motor vehicle towing a trailer, provided the trailer is not equipped with air brakes
  • Class 1, 2, 4 and 6 vehicles as a learner, subject to Alberta licensing requirements
